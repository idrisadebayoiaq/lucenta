# Social Content Plan (X + Instagram)

A daily posting plan for the QuoreStack brand and Lucenta. Each post gets an Instagram design (1080 × 1350) and two captions: one for X (max 280 characters, exactly 2 hashtags) and one for Instagram (long form, up to 20 hashtags).

## How it works

- **Cadence:** 2 posts every day, on X and Instagram.
  - **Morning post (around 9 AM WAT):** the heavier content, such as case studies, build-in-public posts or personal stories.
  - **Evening post (around 7 PM WAT):** the lighter, saveable content, such as tips, lists or quick takes.
- **Mix:** each day pairs two different content pillars so the feed never feels repetitive.
  - **Build in public:** Lucenta progress, features and decisions.
  - **Proof of work:** client case studies and app spotlights.
  - **Education:** tips people save and share.
  - **Personal brand:** the developer behind the work.
- **Style:** every design uses the same system as the first posts. Grid background, blue script word over a bold condensed title, black-and-white cut-out photo, blue accents and the "Let's Connect" footer. Backgrounds alternate white and black across a carousel.
- **Files:** designs, screenshots and captions are produced outside this repo, one folder per post (`<date>-<slug>/`). Personal contact details stay out of this repo.
- **Workflow per post:**
  1. Check the "Needs" column and collect anything missing.
  2. Take any screenshots needed.
  3. Write the captions (X first, then Instagram).
  4. Design the banner or carousel.
  5. Review, adjust, then mark the post **Ready**.
  6. After posting, mark it **Posted** and note anything that performed well.
